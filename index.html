Public App Profile

Spendalf helps a user understand where their money can go safely.

Spendalf is a local-first personal finance agent that reviews account balances, transactions, debts, and bills to produce deterministic, explainable guidance. It is recommend-only and dry-run only. It does not move money, submit payments, or execute transfers on a user's behalf.

What Spendalf Does

Explainable guidance, not autonomous money movement.

Spendalf helps a user monitor cash flow, spot overspending risk, review bills and debts, and generate suggested spending or payoff actions with clear rule traces and confidence scores.

Why Financial Data Is Accessed

To build accurate recommendations from real account activity.

Linked financial data is used to understand balances, transaction history, liabilities, and recurring obligations so the app can estimate safe spending and surface financial risks without inventing missing information.

Data Access Summary

Information Spendalf may access through connected providers

Balances and account metadata

Account names, types, mask values, and current or available balances.

Transaction history

Recent inflows, outflows, merchant descriptions, categories, and timing patterns.

Liabilities and obligations

Debt balances, APRs, minimum payments, due dates, and related bill obligations when available.

User-entered financial records

Manual accounts, debts, bills, and windfalls for institutions without direct connectivity.

Decision Policy

Built to be deterministic and testable.

Core financial recommendations are generated by explicit application logic. Missing or stale data lowers confidence and can block risky recommendations. Optional language-model summaries, when enabled, are limited to explanation and never decide money movement directly.

Single-User Focus

Designed for personal use.

Spendalf is a production-minded personal finance tool for one user. The public information on this site exists so connected institutions and Plaid users can understand who is requesting access and how data is used.
