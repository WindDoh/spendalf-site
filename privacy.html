Privacy & Data Use

How Spendalf uses financial data

Overview

Spendalf is a local-first personal finance application built to help a single user understand balances, spending, bills, and debts. The app uses connected financial data to generate explainable guidance and risk signals.

Data that may be accessed

Depending on the accounts a user connects, Spendalf may access:

  • Account names, types, partial account identifiers, and balance information
  • Transaction details, including dates, descriptions, categories, and amounts
  • Liability information such as balances, APRs, minimum payments, and due dates
  • User-entered financial records such as manual bills, debts, and planned windfalls

How the data is used

  • Estimate safe-to-spend amounts and detect overspending risk
  • Identify upcoming bills, debt obligations, and cash-flow pressure
  • Generate recommended actions with confidence scores, blockers, and rule traces
  • Support optional summaries or explanations when enabled by the user

What Spendalf does not do

  • It does not initiate ACH transfers, debt payments, or brokerage trades
  • It does not let a language model make money-movement decisions
  • It does not invent missing balances, APRs, due dates, or stale financial data

Third-party services

Spendalf may rely on Plaid for financial connectivity. Optional explanation features may use a language-model provider only when explicitly enabled. Core financial policy logic remains deterministic and outside the language-model layer.
